INTANGIBLE ASSET RISK 无形资产风险Intangible Asset Risk

知识产权调查取证Intellectual Property Investigation and Evidence Collection

知识产权往往是企业最具价值、也最难核实的资产之一。商标仿冒、专利侵权、商业秘密外流等问题，多数情况下不会主动浮出水面——发现的滞后本身，即是一种代价。Intellectual property is often a company's most valuable asset — and one of the hardest to keep watch over. Counterfeiting, patent infringement and leaked trade secrets rarely announce themselves; the time it takes to notice is, in itself, a cost.

EVIDENCE & COORDINATION 取证与协同Evidence & coordination

取证与协同Evidence & coordination
