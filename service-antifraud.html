ANTI-FRAUD INVESTIGATION 反舞弊调查Anti-Fraud Investigation

反舞弊调查Anti-Fraud Investigation

舞弊的行为是隐性的和长期存在的，舞弊者一般会采取必要的反措施以掩盖自己的舞弊行为——例如受贿钱款不汇入名下账号、代理采购或飞单的公司由他人注册等。舞弊案件的调查需要在严格保密的情况下进行，由资深调查专家全程操控，涵盖策划、执行、反馈、调整与继续深入等环节。Fraud is often deliberately concealed — a bribe routed to an account that isn't in the fraudster's name, a shadow company registered through a proxy. Investigating it calls for strict confidentiality and hands-on control by senior investigators from start to finish: planning, execution, feedback, adjustment, and following the thread wherever it leads.

佑信在介入舞弊案件时，会与委托公司签署保密协议，严格执行保密要求，并根据客户最终需求——如中止职务、追回损失、法院诉讼或刑事侦察——来推进相应的取证工作。We sign a confidentiality agreement with the client and shape the evidence-gathering around their objective — whether that means suspending an employee, recovering losses, pursuing litigation, or referring the matter to the authorities.
