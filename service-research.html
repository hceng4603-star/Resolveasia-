INDUSTRY RESEARCH 行业及企业研究Industry & company research

行业及企业研究Industry & company research

根据高需求领域，我们成立了不同行业的专有调研组，包括医疗医药保健、金融保险投资、互联网及服务、能源矿产环保等。企业调研维度包括产品质量研究、服务质量研究、企业形象研究与产品外延研究。Dedicated research teams cover high-demand sectors including healthcare/pharma, financial services, internet/tech, and energy/resources — assessing product quality, service quality, brand image and product extension.

USER & BRAND 用户与品牌研究User & brand research

用户与品牌研究User & brand research

通过调查和访谈，深入分析消费者的心理以及行为习惯的形成原因，帮助委托方提炼有效影响消费者消费决策的各种因素，为营销策略制定提供依据——涵盖消费者行为态度、产品/服务测试、宣传促销效果评估、品牌研究与NPS研究。Through surveys and interviews, we get at the psychology and behavior behind purchase decisions, helping clients understand what actually drives them — spanning consumer attitudes, product and service testing, campaign effectiveness, brand research and NPS.

QUALITY CONTROL 质控机制Quality control

质控机制Quality control

从调研过程、沟通汇报、最终交付物三个方面进行质量控制：调研全程线上实时监控、录音笔录、委托方可跟访；项目实行经理负责制，可随时沟通；最终交付关键联系人清单、调研录音、大纲原稿、数据分析与可视化报告。Quality is controlled at three stages — in the field, in how we communicate, and in what we deliver: interviews are recorded and monitored in real time, with clients free to observe; a dedicated project manager keeps communication ongoing; and the final package includes contact lists, recordings, research outlines, data analysis and a visualized report.
