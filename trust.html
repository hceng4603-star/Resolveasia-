COMPLIANCE 合规保密Compliance

我们以合法合规的方式开展调查We conduct every investigation lawfully

佑信承诺，所有商业调查工作以合法合规的方式开展，严格遵守《网络安全法》《个人信息保护法》《国家安全法》等相关法律法规。佑信团队本身在行内有10年+经验，对于项目的合规、敏感度有很好的判断。当我们自身无法充分判断时，则会咨询外部法律团队进行进一步判断。在整个调查过程中，佑信会将任何风险与客户保持充分沟通。Resolve is committed to conducting every investigation lawfully, in strict compliance with China's Cybersecurity Law, Personal Information Protection Law and National Security Law. With more than a decade in the field, our team has sound judgment on where the compliance lines sit — and we bring in outside legal counsel whenever a situation calls for it, keeping clients informed of any risk along the way.

CONFIDENTIALITY SAFEGUARDS 保密机制Confidentiality safeguards

保密机制Confidentiality safeguards

01

信息保密：仅对接人员知情，内部以无关联壳公司代指客户Information confidentiality: only your point of contact is briefed; internally, each client is referred to by the name of an unrelated shell company

02

往来保密：专职同事处理合同与财务往来，可接受海外账户支付Transaction confidentiality: dedicated staff handle contracts and payments; overseas account payment accepted

03

专人对接：项目执行同事不向其他同事披露项目内容与进展Dedicated handling: project staff don't disclose details to colleagues outside the project

04

资料保密：可通过加密云盘传送报告，设置链接有效期与查阅记录Document confidentiality: reports delivered via encrypted cloud storage, with link expiry dates and access logs

05

沟通保密：使用第三方会议软件沟通，不暴露客户信息Communication confidentiality: third-party conferencing tools that do not expose client identity

06

保密培训：入职、离职及每季度定期开展保密培训Confidentiality training: at onboarding, offboarding, and every quarter

佑信咨询取得涉外调查许可证、美国背景调查协会（PBSA）成员、中国科技咨询协会注册单位会员等相关资质，我们依法依规开展各项工作。当我们自身无法充分判断项目合规边界时，会主动咨询外部法律团队进行进一步判断，确保客户始终掌握项目中的合规状态。Resolve holds a Foreign-Related Investigation License, is a member of PBSA, and is a registered member of CATCC — and we operate strictly within the law. Where a compliance boundary is unclear, we bring in outside legal counsel before proceeding, keeping clients informed at every step.
